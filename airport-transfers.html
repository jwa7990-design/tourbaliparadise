Prices are one way and per vehicle, not per person, and include fuel, parking and toll roads. Your price is confirmed on WhatsApp before you book.

How it works

  • Send your flight number, the number of passengers and bags, and where you’re staying.
  • Aris confirms the price and the pickup arrangements on WhatsApp.
  • He meets you at Ngurah Rai International Airport and drives you to your hotel or villa.
  • Leaving Bali? The same prices apply from your hotel to the airport.

Before you fly

Most visitors need a visa, the free All Indonesia arrival card and the Bali tourist levy. See Bali entry requirements and official links so you only use the official websites.

Finding Aris when you land

When you land, connect to the airport Wi-Fi and send Aris a WhatsApp message. He’ll reply with a photo of exactly where he’s waiting and keep you updated until you meet. There’s no need to look for a name sign in the crowd.

Arriving and leaving

Include your flight details when you enquire so the pickup can be arranged around your arrival. Let Aris know how many bags you’re bringing; a Toyota Innova takes up to 7 passengers, and 14-seat Hiace vans are available for groups.

Many visitors combine a transfer with a day out. You can be collected at the airport and go straight into a day with Aris, or book a transfer on the same day as a tour.

Questions

Is the price per person?

No. Airport transfer prices are per vehicle, one way: a Toyota Innova for up to 7 passengers or a 14-seat Hiace for groups.

What is included?

Fuel, parking and toll roads are included in the price.

How do I find my driver at the airport?

When you land, connect to the airport Wi-Fi and send Aris a WhatsApp message. He’ll reply with a photo of exactly where he’s waiting and keep you updated until you meet.

Can you take me to the airport as well?

Yes. Departure transfers from your hotel or villa to the airport cost the same as arrivals.

My area isn’t listed. Can you still help?

Yes. Send your address and Aris will give you a price.

Plan with Aris

Message Aris on WhatsApp. He replies personally and confirms the price before you book.
