Airport Transfers

Arrive and depart with ease. Aris can meet you at Bali's I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) and take you to your hotel or villa, or get you back to the airport in good time for your flight.

  • Airport pickups when you land
  • Hotel and villa transfers
  • Arrival coordination: share your flight details so pickup can be arranged around your arrival
  • Departure transfers back to the airport
  • Luggage arrangements: let Aris know how many bags you're bringing

1:1 Scooter Tours

Travelling solo? See Bali the way many locals get around: on the back of a scooter. Aris rides and you sit behind him, so you can simply enjoy the views. It's a relaxed, one-to-one way to explore, with a helmet provided.

  • One-to-one: just you and Aris
  • You ride as a passenger, Aris rides
  • Helmet provided
  • Ideal for solo travellers
  • A route planned around what you'd like to see
