Bali Through Local Eyes

See Bali the way Aris does. Not a list of attractions, but the places he actually goes and the everyday life most visitors drive straight past.

  • Full day · about 8–9 hours
  • Private · 1–4 guests
  • By car

Is it for you?

  • First time in Bali
  • Curious about culture
  • Couples and small groups
  • Anyone who’s done the main sights

Included

  • Private car with fuel, parking and tolls
  • Market tastings and warung coffee
  • Temple donations and sarong hire
  • Water
  • Pickup in South Bali or Ubud

Not included

  • Lunch
  • Entry tickets at paid attractions
  • Anything you choose to buy

Good to know

  • Wear clothes that cover shoulders and knees for temples
  • Ceremony days can change the route; Aris will plan around them
  • Comfortable shoes, there’s some walking
