Price: airport to Uluwatu

Airport ↔ UluwatuOne way, per car. Fuel, parking and tolls included.

The same price applies in both directions, from the airport to your hotel or from your hotel back to the airport.

How long does it take?

The drive between the airport and Uluwatu usually takes about 45 minutes to 1 hour, depending on traffic. Traffic around the airport and in South Bali is heaviest in the late afternoon and evening, so allow extra time for departures.

About Uluwatu

Uluwatu is on the Bukit Peninsula at Bali’s southern tip, known for its cliffs, surf beaches and the clifftop Uluwatu Temple. Villas and hotels are spread out along small roads, so it helps to share your exact address.

Questions

How much is a taxi from Bali airport to Uluwatu?

With Aris, the airport to Uluwatu transfer is a fixed price per car, shown on this page, with fuel, parking and tolls included.

How long is the drive from the airport to Uluwatu?

Usually about 45 minutes to 1 hour, depending on traffic.

Is the price per person?

No. It’s per car, one way. Cars take up to 4 or 6 passengers depending on the car arranged.

When you land, connect to the airport Wi-Fi and send Aris a WhatsApp message. He’ll reply with a photo of exactly where he’s waiting and keep you updated until you meet.

Can I book the return trip too?

Yes. Departures from your hotel to the airport are the same price.
