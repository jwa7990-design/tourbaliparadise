Kintamani Coffee & Volcano

The highlands north of Ubud, where Bali’s arabica grows on the slopes of an active volcano. Easy to love, easy to photograph, and better with someone who knows the farmers.

  • Full day · about 8–9 hours
  • Private · 1–4 guests
  • By car or scooter

Is it for you?

  • Coffee lovers
  • Photographers
  • Couples and small groups
  • Solo travellers (scooter or car)

Included

  • Private car with fuel, parking and tolls
  • Coffee tasting and a coffee at the viewpoint
  • Kintamani entry fees
  • Water

Not included

  • Lunch
  • Optional extra stops with their own tickets

Good to know

  • Bring a light jacket, the highlands are cooler
  • Clear mornings have the best views
  • A half-day version is possible from Ubud only
