Kintamani coffee and volcano day trip

The Kintamani highlands are home to Mount Batur, Lake Batur and some of Bali’s best-known coffee. Spend the day there with Aris, at your own pace.

Plan a Kintamani day

Why Kintamani?

Kintamani is the high ground in north-east Bali, around the rim of a huge volcanic caldera. Inside it sit Mount Batur, an active volcano, and Lake Batur, the largest lake in Bali. The views across the caldera are some of the most striking on the island.

The volcanic soil and cooler air are why the area grows coffee. Kintamani arabica is one of Indonesia’s recognised regional coffees.

A typical day

  • A scenic drive north through villages and rice fields.
  • A visit to a local coffee producer to see how the coffee is grown and processed, with a tasting.
  • Coffee at a viewpoint looking across the caldera to Mount Batur and the lake.
  • Lunch with the volcano in view.
  • One extra stop of your choice: Tegallalang rice terraces, a temple, or a soak at a natural hot spring by the lake.

Every day is planned with you on WhatsApp beforehand, so you can change the stops.

How long does it take?

From Ubud, Kintamani is roughly 1 to 1.5 hours’ drive. From South Bali (Seminyak, Canggu, Kuta, Sanur) allow about 2 hours or more each way, so it works best as a full day. Aris will suggest timings for where you’re staying.

Tips for the highlands

  • Bring a light jacket. The highlands are noticeably cooler than the coast.
  • Mornings are usually clearest. Cloud and mist often roll in later in the day.
  • About civet coffee (kopi luwak): many guests prefer to avoid it because of animal-welfare concerns with caged civets. You don’t need it to enjoy Kintamani coffee.

Questions

Is Kintamani worth visiting?

For many visitors, yes: the caldera views of Mount Batur and Lake Batur, the cooler air and the coffee make it one of the most memorable days in Bali.

Can I visit Kintamani as a half day?

From Ubud, yes. From South Bali it’s better as a full day because of the travel time.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes and a light jacket, plus clothes that cover shoulders and knees if you’d like to visit a temple.
