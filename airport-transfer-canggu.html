Price: airport to Canggu

Airport ↔ CangguOne way, per car. Fuel, parking and tolls included.

The same price applies in both directions, from the airport to your hotel or from your hotel back to the airport.

How long does it take?

The drive between the airport and Canggu usually takes about 45 minutes to 1.5 hours, depending on traffic. Traffic around the airport and in South Bali is heaviest in the late afternoon and evening, so allow extra time for departures.

About Canggu

Canggu is on the west coast, north of Seminyak, known for its beaches, surf and cafés. The roads into Canggu are narrow and get busy, so travel times vary a lot through the day.

From Canggu, a full day with Aris can head inland to rice terraces and villages, or north to the highlands.

Booking your transfer

  • Send your flight number, passenger numbers and how many bags you have.
  • Include your hotel or villa name in Canggu.
  • Aris confirms the price and pickup arrangements on WhatsApp.
  • Fuel, parking and toll roads are included.

Questions

How long is the drive from the airport to Canggu?

No. It’s per car, one way. Cars take up to 4 or 6 passengers depending on the car arranged.

Can I book the return trip too?

Yes. Departures from your hotel to the airport are the same price.
