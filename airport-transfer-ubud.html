Bali airport to Ubud

Price: airport to Ubud

Airport ↔ UbudOne way, per car. Fuel, parking and tolls included.

The same price applies in both directions, from the airport to your hotel or from your hotel back to the airport.

How long does it take?

The drive between the airport and Ubud usually takes about 1 to 1.5 hours, and sometimes longer in heavy traffic. Traffic around the airport and in South Bali is heaviest in the late afternoon and evening, so allow extra time for departures.

About Ubud

Ubud sits inland among rice fields and river valleys, north of the airport. It’s the furthest of the popular areas from the airport, so a pre-arranged private car saves negotiating at arrivals after a long flight.

Ubud is also the easiest base for a day with Aris: villages, temples, markets and the Kintamani highlands are all within reach.

Booking your transfer

  • Send your flight number, passenger numbers and how many bags you have.
  • Include your hotel or villa name in Ubud.
  • Aris confirms the price and pickup arrangements on WhatsApp.
  • When you land, connect to the airport Wi-Fi and message Aris. He’ll send a photo of where he’s waiting and keep you updated.
  • Fuel, parking and toll roads are included.

Questions

How much is a taxi from Bali airport to Ubud?

With Aris, the airport to Ubud transfer is a fixed price per car, shown on this page, with fuel, parking and tolls included.

How long is the drive from the airport to Ubud?

Usually about 1 to 1.5 hours, and sometimes longer in heavy traffic.

Is the price per person?

No. It’s per car, one way. Cars take up to 4 or 6 passengers depending on the car arranged.

How will I find Aris at the airport?

When you land, connect to the airport Wi-Fi and send Aris a WhatsApp message. He’ll reply with a photo of exactly where he’s waiting and keep you updated until you meet.

Can I book the return trip too?

Yes. Departures from your hotel to the airport are the same price.
