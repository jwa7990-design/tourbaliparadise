    FAQs

    How do I arrange a driver?

    Send Aris a message on WhatsApp, or fill in the enquiry form, with your dates, where you're staying and what you'd like to do. He'll reply personally to check availability and talk through the details.

    Can I customise my day?

    Yes. You choose where to go and how long to stay. Aris can suggest places if you'd like ideas, and plans can change on the day where time allows.

    How much does a private driver cost?

    It depends on how long you need a driver and where you'd like to go. See the indicative prices on the Our Services page. Aris confirms the final price before you book.

    Can Aris collect us from the airport?

    Yes. Airport pickups and drop-offs are one of the main services. Include your flight details when you enquire.

    Can he recommend places to visit?

    Yes. Tell him what you enjoy, such as food, nature, culture or beaches, and he'll suggest places that suit you.

    Are attraction tickets included?

    No. Entry tickets, activities and meals aren't included, and you pay the venues directly. Fuel, parking and toll roads are included in Aris's price.

    Can families book?

    Yes, families are welcome. Let Aris know how many adults and children are travelling, and the children's ages, so the right arrangements can be made. If you need a child seat, ask when you enquire.

    How is my booking confirmed?

    Your booking is confirmed only when Aris confirms it with you personally on WhatsApp, including the final price. Sending an enquiry through this website doesn't create a booking or take payment.
