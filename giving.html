How it works

  1. 1

    You book a trip

    An airport transfer, a day out or a scooter tour. Your price stays exactly the same.

  2. 2

    Aris sets money aside

    A fixed amount from every trip goes into the giving fund.

  3. 3

    It goes to a local cause

    The fund is donated directly to one Bali cause chosen by Aris.

  4. 4

    We publish the proof

    Every donation is listed below with its date, amount and receipt.

    Questions

    Does it cost me extra?

    No. The donation comes out of Aris's price, so you pay the published price and nothing more.

    How do I know the money is really donated?

    Every donation is listed on this page with the date, the amount and a photo of the receipt.

    Which cause do you support?

    Aris is choosing one local Bali cause to support. Once the organisation has agreed, it will be named on this page.

    Can I donate too?

    Yes. Once the cause is named here, you can donate to them directly through their own website.
