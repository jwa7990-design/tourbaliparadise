Before you fly

Visa, arrival card and tourist levy: get them from the official sites.

Copycat websites charge visitors extra for things that are cheap or even free. Here’s what most travellers need, with the official links.

Entry requirements and official links
  1. 01

    Visa on arrival

    Apply online before you fly (e-VOA), or at the airport.

  2. 02

    All Indonesia arrival card

    Customs and health form. Free, online, within 3 days of arriving.

  3. 03

    Bali tourist levy

    Rp 150,000 per person, paid online or on arrival.
