Explore Bali

Bali's interior is a patchwork of rice terraces, jungle valleys, crater lakes and temples. A day of sightseeing usually means a few unhurried stops, with time to walk, take photos and have lunch along the way.

  • Tegallalang Rice Terraces Terraced rice fields in a valley north of Ubud.
  • Ulun Danu Beratan A lakeside water temple in the cool Bedugul highlands.
  • Uluwatu Temple A clifftop temple on the Bukit Peninsula, popular at sunset.
  • Sidemen A quiet valley in East Bali with rice fields and mountain views.
  • Kerta Gosa The historic court pavilion in Klungkung, known for its painted ceilings.
  • Waterfalls Tegenungan, Tibumana and others within reach of Ubud.

02

Food & Coffee

Some of the best meals in Bali come from small, family-run warungs. Try nasi campur, sate lilit or babi guling, wander through a morning market, or visit a coffee plantation to see how Bali coffee is grown and roasted.

  • Local warungs Simple, family-run eateries serving Balinese and Indonesian dishes.
  • Coffee plantations Tasting stops in the Kintamani highlands and around Ubud.
  • Traditional markets Fresh produce, spices and offerings at local morning markets.
  • Cooking classes Learn to cook Balinese dishes with an independent cooking school.
  • Jimbaran seafood Grilled seafood at tables on the sand at Jimbaran Bay.
  • Cafés Relaxed café culture in Ubud, Canggu and Seminyak.

03

Adventure

For a more active day, Bali has rivers to raft, jungle trails to ride and a volcano to climb before sunrise. These activities are run by independent operators. Aris can help you choose one and get you there.

  • ATV rides Muddy jungle and rice-field trails, mostly around Ubud.
  • White-water rafting On the Ayung River near Ubud or the Telaga Waja in East Bali.
  • Mount Batur sunrise trek A guided early-morning climb up an active volcano.
  • Water sports Jet skis, parasailing and more at Tanjung Benoa.
  • Surf lessons Beginner-friendly waves at Kuta and Canggu.
  • Snorkelling & diving Clear water around Amed, Padang Bai and Menjangan Island.

04

Family Fun

Travelling with children? Bali has plenty to keep everyone happy, from water slides to calm, shallow beaches. Let Aris know your children's ages and he can help you plan a day with sensible travel times.

  • Waterbom Bali A large water park in Kuta with slides for all ages.
  • Bali Zoo Animal encounters in Singapadu, south of Ubud.
  • Sanur Beach Calm, reef-protected water that suits younger swimmers.
  • Ubud Monkey Forest A forest sanctuary home to long-tailed macaques. Keep bags zipped!
  • Turtle conservation The Turtle Conservation and Education Center on Serangan Island.
  • Nusa Dua Calm beaches and a paved beachfront walkway.

05

Beaches & Sunsets

Bali's west-facing coast is made for sunsets. Watch the sky change from a clifftop at Uluwatu, have dinner with your feet in the sand at Jimbaran, or find a quieter beach in the south.

  • Uluwatu Clifftop views, surf beaches and the evening Kecak dance at the temple.
  • Jimbaran Bay Seafood dinners on the beach as the sun goes down.
  • Tanah Lot A sea temple on a rocky outcrop, best seen at sunset.
  • Melasti Beach White sand below limestone cliffs on the Bukit Peninsula.
  • Seminyak Beach clubs and sunset bars along the sand.
  • Sanur Bali's sunrise side, with a long beachfront path.

Culture & Local Life

Balinese Hindu culture is part of everyday life here: offerings at every doorway, ceremonies in village temples and crafts passed down through families. Please visit respectfully. A sarong and sash are required at temples.

  • Tirta Empul A water temple near Tampaksiring, known for its holy springs.
  • Besakih Bali's largest temple complex, on the slopes of Mount Agung.
  • Penglipuran A traditional village in Bangli, known for its neat layout and bamboo forest.
  • Craft villages Silverwork in Celuk, woodcarving in Mas and painting in Batuan.
  • Traditional dance Evening Legong and Kecak performances in Ubud and at Uluwatu.
  • Ceremonies Temple festivals and processions happen throughout the year. If you come across one, watch quietly from the side.
